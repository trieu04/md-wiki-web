# Contribution quality and own results

A contribution can be true and still lower the quality of the wiki. Review quality together with correctness.

## Reject or request revision when a contribution

- adds a claim without declared evidence, or cites an existing footnote that does not support the new claim;
- removes sourced content without a valid stated reason;
- makes a precise statement vaguer, or adds weasel wording ("some researchers believe", "it is widely known");
- adds promotional, peacock or editorial language, first-person text, advice or how-to instructions;
- adds content that belongs on another page, or repeats what the page already says;
- breaks Markdown (wiki links, footnote markers, lists, tables, headings), adds spelling or grammar errors, or
  writes in another language than the page.

A reason that understates the change ("copyedit" on a change of content) is itself a reason to request revision.

## Own experimental results

Results from the contributor's or their lab's own unpublished experiments belong only in a `## Lab notes` section
at the end of the page body, never in the article body (lead, other sections, lists, tables), even when they are
correct and well documented. Each note:

- gives the date, the lab or role, the sample and conditions, the number of replicates and the result with its
  spread;
- links to a versioned data page in the wiki (for example `lab-data/...md`) that is declared as a source;
- matches that data page, does not generalise beyond its conditions, and does not replace or silently contradict
  a sourced claim in the body.

Reject an own result placed in the body, a note without a declared data page, and a note that misreports its
data page or generalises beyond it.
